Hi Nadia, no worries at all, and good luck with the new launches!

Short answer: **yes, it'll be tracked, and you don't need to wait for me.** Codefuel revenue is matched
to the ad by the `gd` and `r` parameters in the landing URL, not by which account the campaign runs
in. So a Codefuel domain on SSM9 gets picked up the same way as on your other Codefuel accounts.

A few things to keep in mind:

1. **Use the same URL setup as your other Codefuel campaigns.** The landing URL needs the `gd=` and
   `r=` parameters, exactly like your existing Codefuel ads.
2. **Please use new gd/r combinations.** Don't reuse ones that are already running in another
   account. If the same pair is live in two accounts, Codefuel reports it once, and the dashboard
   has to assign all of that revenue to one of them.
3. **First-day timing.** New Outbrain ads are picked up in our overnight sync. On launch day, the
   Codefuel revenue might show for a few hours under "Unattributed Outbrain". After the next
   morning's sync it moves onto the right campaign. Totals are correct the whole time. Codefuel also
   reports about 3–6 hours late, so same-day numbers will fill in as the day goes on.

One thing I need from you: **SSM9 is currently labelled as a DDC account.** That label controls the
Project filter and the Accounts tab. If we leave it, the Codefuel revenue will be counted correctly
but will show under "DDC". I'd suggest switching SSM9 to Codefuel. It's a one-minute change on my side.
The only side effect is on SSM9's past DDC numbers. The DDC revenue that never matched a specific ad
(most of it) would move to a separate "DDC – unassigned" line instead of sitting under SSM9.
Overall totals don't change at all. Shall I switch it?

Also, since DDC is paused, do you want me to **stop the daily DDC Type-Tag email**? It will just be
empty from now on.

For what it's worth, the dashboard agrees with you on DDC: SSM9 spent about $8.9k against $6.8k of
DDC revenue in its first six weeks, and it was still around −76% margin in October. Pausing it looks
like the right call.
