# Reply to Nadia — campaign attribution fixed (drafted 2026-09-09)

Hi Nadia,

Fixed and live — you were right, it was a real bug on our side.

Around 28 August your tracking template started adding the publisher ID onto the end of the type
tag. We were matching the tag exactly, so from then on more than half the ads couldn't claim their
own revenue — it drifted onto older ads and got spread across campaigns that weren't running.

On the day you sent me (7 Sept): 21th July V1 was $20.80 with no traffic, now shows nothing.
5th Aug V2 was $12.24, now $0.00. That money moved to the campaigns that earned it —
**Machinery Manufacturers $41.60 on 171 clicks**, **Insurance Quotes $24.47 on 124 clicks**.
The day's total is unchanged at $276.22.

Two things to expect:

- **Your DDC history back to 22 July has been recalculated** — about a quarter of the revenue has
  moved between campaigns. Daily and account totals haven't changed and still match DDC to the cent,
  but individual campaigns will differ from last week's screenshots.
- **A small amount may show on a campaign with no clicks that day** — e.g. Pest Control, $0.06 on the
  7th, which had 10 clicks on the 6th. People often search the day after they click, so it's credited
  back to the campaign that bought the traffic. That one's correct.
